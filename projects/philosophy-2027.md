A year-long reading and writing project through major problems in Western philosophy, with particular attention to science, medicine, neuroscience, consciousness, morality, mortality, and the good life.

The aim is not to read as many philosophers as possible, but to spend sustained time with a smaller group of thinkers and questions.

## Prologue

Before beginning the chronological project, I am starting with a short introduction to philosophy and to the activity of philosophical thinking itself.

- Bertrand Russell — **“The Value of Philosophy”**
- Edward Craig — ***Philosophy: A Very Short Introduction***
- Martin Heidegger — ***What Is Called Thinking?***
- Hannah Arendt — ***The Life of the Mind, Vol. I: Thinking***

The Heidegger and Arendt readings overlap with a philosophy-of-technology book club and will be revisited later in the year.
